# Destination Activation Standards

## Purpose

A destination becomes public only when a bounded catalog is useful, lawful, safe, fresh, and maintainable. These thresholds are intentionally achievable by a solo founder and do not imply comprehensive destination coverage.

## Required launch boundary

- Approved polygon or explicit administrative/locality boundary
- Separate emergency-service radius where essential facilities lie outside the traveler boundary
- Clear in-product coverage statement
- Nationwide-compatible geographic identifiers

## Minimum content thresholds

| Domain | Minimum publishable coverage |
|---|---:|
| Verified attractions and hidden gems | 30 total, including at least 10 non-flagship/local experiences |
| Verified restaurants | 40 across at least 6 relevant cuisine/dietary categories; no paid ranking |
| Food specialties | 10 with culturally reviewed descriptions |
| Current events and recurring markets/walking streets | 8 active items, including at least 3 recurring items; if seasonally unavailable, approved evergreen activity substitutes must be disclosed |
| Local activities | 12 verified activities distinct from ordinary attraction listings |
| Hospitals | All major relevant hospitals in the service radius, minimum 3 if available |
| Clinics | Minimum 5 verified facilities, with no unsupported capability claims |
| Pharmacies | Minimum 10 geographically distributed verified facilities |
| Police | Relevant station coverage plus verified national police number |
| Tourist Police/assistance | Relevant station or hotline and TAT/tourist-assistance path |
| Rescue/medical emergency | Verified national EMS number and locally relevant official coverage |
| Fire | Verified national fire number and relevant local station data where authoritative |

Numerical thresholds do not override quality. If a category has fewer legitimate records, activate only with a founder-approved limitation and clear user communication; never invent or pad the catalog.

## Provenance and rights thresholds

- 100% of public records pass required record-level provenance.
- 100% of critical emergency assertions have approved assertion-level provenance.
- At least 95% of ordinary volatile assertions have source, last checked, and verification state; the remainder must be omitted rather than published unsupported.
- 100% of displayed images pass license and subject verification.
- 100% of required attributions render correctly in review.
- No expired, takedown, or permission-uncertain data or media.

## Image coverage

- At least 50% of flagship attraction and restaurant records have authorized documentary images.
- Remaining records use neutral category presentation, not AI substitutes.
- AI-generated imagery appears only in approved decorative contexts.
- The destination may launch with lower image coverage if content remains understandable and the founder explicitly accepts it; rights quality cannot be relaxed.

## Translation and search

- 100% of names, addresses, categories, emergency labels, dates/statuses, and core actions support the approved English/Thai presentation.
- 100% of emergency fixed text receives fluent human review.
- At least 90% of catalog records have an approved English summary; missing summaries use structured facts, not live unreviewed translation.
- Search evaluation covers Thai canonical names, English aliases, common transliterations, duplicate names, and geographic disambiguation.

## Freshness

- 100% of critical emergency assertions are within the approved cadence.
- At least 95% of ordinary volatile assertions are within their cadence.
- 100% of event occurrences are checked within seven days of publication and again close to the event according to risk.
- Stale critical fields are suppressed automatically or operationally before launch.
- Review queue can be cleared by the founder within the approved operating schedule.

## Correction readiness

- Private report path works for every public record.
- Critical emergency correction procedure has been exercised.
- License expiry and image takedown have been simulated.
- Founder can suppress a record or assertion without a deployment.
- Revision and audit evidence are recoverable.
- Public support/privacy contact exists.

## AI quality thresholds

On an approved English/Thai destination evaluation set:

- 100% of referenced real entities resolve to approved internal records.
- 0 fabricated phone numbers, addresses, emergency services, events, or opening hours in the release candidate.
- At least 95% citation-to-claim validity for material factual claims.
- At least 90% itinerary constraint adherence.
- 100% safe fallback on missing or stale emergency evidence.
- Prompt-injection and provider-failure tests pass agreed critical cases.

Any critical fabrication blocks activation regardless of aggregate score.

## Cost and resilience thresholds

- Seven-day pilot load test projects below 70% of the monthly hard ceiling.
- Per-user, per-device/session, global, token, response, and itinerary limits operate as designed.
- AI and optional provider kill switches are tested.
- Verified records and emergency search remain available during AI outage.
- Backup restoration and takedown re-application are tested.

## Activation decision

The founder records:

- Boundary and activation date
- Evidence for every threshold
- Accepted limitations and residual risks
- Next review date
- Rollback owner and procedure

No traffic, marketing, or commercial target can waive emergency, license, provenance, or AI fabrication blockers.
