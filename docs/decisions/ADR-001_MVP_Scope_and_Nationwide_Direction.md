# ADR-001 — MVP Scope and Nationwide Direction

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-08-01
- Decision owner: Founder
- Applies to: Version 1.0 implementation and content operations

## Context

AI Travel Companion Thailand Version 1.0 launches only when all 77 provinces meet the approved minimum launch-coverage criteria. A solo founder still needs an incremental internal verification process without weakening provenance, licensing, emergency-safety, privacy, or cost controls.

## Decision

1. Version 1.0 launches only after all 77 provinces meet the approved minimum verified coverage criteria. Nationwide schema and UI foundations do not by themselves satisfy this launch gate.
2. Real destinations and records may be verified and activated incrementally in non-production environments. Public production launch remains blocked until the 77-province launch gate is met, and every visible record must pass publication, evidence, source-rights, verification, translation, freshness, and suppression gates.
3. Travelers use the application for free. AI quota exhaustion never removes deterministic catalog, trip, map-handoff, or verified help features.
4. The MVP has no internal hotel booking, checkout, payment, reservation inventory, or hotel-management system.
5. The MVP has no internal turn-by-turn navigation, route computation, traffic engine, or route optimization. Google Maps and Apple Maps remain external handoffs generated by the application.
6. Partner portals, billing, sponsored ranking, campaigns, packages, and promotions are post-MVP and require separate product, legal, privacy, security, and trust approval.
7. Emergency and traveler-assistance results can never be sponsored, commercially reordered, or made less visible because of payment.
8. Live AI remains disabled until the eligible catalog, anonymous sessions, traveler-controlled trip editor, quota enforcement, usage accounting, provider budget, provider terms, and safe fallback are all verified.
9. Organic ranking cannot be changed by payment. Any future commercial placement must be explicitly labeled and kept outside emergency results.

## Consequences

- The 77-province import remains real-classification but unpublished and evidence-pending.
- Internal development may continue with synthetic fixtures and unpublished real identity scaffolds.
- Missing Phase 1B evidence blocks real publication and coverage claims, not schema, CMS, API, or UI development.
- The current consolidated implementation batch is limited to secure content operations, authenticated Admin boundaries, reproducible schema verification, reusable UI foundations, and automated quality controls.

## Explicit exclusions for the current batch

- Partner platform or portal
- Billing or payment
- Sponsored content or ranking
- Campaigns, packages, or promotions
- Live AI provider
- Native mobile application
- Hotel booking
- Internal navigation or route optimization
- Production deployment
- Unlicensed real-place imagery or unverified real emergency contacts

## Supersession

This record clarifies execution priority without rewriting historical architecture or evidence documents. A later change to these boundaries requires a new ADR and Founder approval.
