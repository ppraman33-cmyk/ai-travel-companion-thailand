## Evidence decision

The three sources jointly support the 25 district identity records in the source-controlled registry. The Department of Provincial Administration register is authoritative for codes and Thai names. The provincial source independently confirms the count and provincial relationship. The university reference is supporting evidence for English names, not a replacement for the government sources.

No attraction, restaurant, event, local-food, market, activity, emergency, healthcare, police, rescue, transport, or producer record is approved by this register. Those facts require assertion-level evidence from the responsible primary source.

## Known limitations

- Administrative boundary geometry has not been imported. The district-to-province relationship is verified, while polygon/coordinate validation remains a documented gap.
- Source usage is limited to rewritten factual assertions. No copied descriptions or pages are stored.
- Retrieval did not establish reusable media rights.
- Restaurant hours, event occurrences, emergency contacts, and service availability are time-sensitive and require their own freshness rules.
