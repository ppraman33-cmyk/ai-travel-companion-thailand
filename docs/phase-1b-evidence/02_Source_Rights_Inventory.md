# Source Rights Inventory

## Publication rule

**No record or assertion may be published unless minimum provenance, authorization, verification, and freshness requirements pass.** Public accessibility is not permission. Google Maps, Facebook, general web search, unauthorized directories, and scraped websites are not content sources.

## Status vocabulary

Use exactly: **Proposed**, **Evidence needed**, **Partially verified**, **Verified**, **Rejected**, or **Expired**.

`Verified` requires concrete evidence supporting the exact intended use, a reviewer, and a current recheck date. Legal interpretation may still require qualified review.

## Minimum publication evidence

- Identified owner or authority
- Stable source reference
- Evidence supporting storage and public display
- Modification/translation rights where those actions occur
- Commercial-use position suitable for future monetization
- Required attribution and caching behavior
- Provider/access limitations
- Acquisition, verification, and recheck dates
- Exit and takedown process
- Assertion-level evidence for volatile or consequential facts
- Qualified legal review when terms are ambiguous

## Decision boundary

Codex may organize evidence and recommend a status. The founder or designated rights reviewer approves the source. Qualified counsel resolves legal ambiguity. Empty templates and summaries alone cannot produce `Verified` status.
