# Smoke Season and Service Risk Policy

## Status

- Policy status: **Proposed**
- Evidence obtained: Phase 1 identified seasonal air-quality risk
- Evidence needed: qualified health/safety review, authoritative Thai air-quality sources, provider terms, historical operating analysis, and founder decision
- Live weather and air-quality integration: excluded from the first thin vertical slice

## Purpose

Set safe product behavior when Chiang Mai experiences smoke, haze, or degraded air quality without presenting the product as a medical authority or inventing real-time conditions.

## Source policy

Before displaying current air quality, approve:

- Responsible official Thai authority source
- Measurement locations and update frequency
- Metric and scale definitions
- License, attribution, storage, caching, and commercial-use rights
- Provider outage and discrepancy behavior
- Qualified review of traveler-facing health language

No current air-quality source is approved by this document. General forecasts, social posts, visual appearance, or AI model memory are not sufficient.

## First thin-slice behavior

Without separately approved live integration, the app may:

- Show a dated, reviewed seasonal-information article.
- Explain that conditions vary by day and location.
- Link to an approved authoritative external source after rights/link review.
- Encourage travelers to seek official guidance and qualified medical advice.
- Allow the founder to post a manually reviewed service notice with source and expiry.

The app must not present a live AQI, current safety status, or personalized exposure conclusion without current approved data.

## AI boundaries

AI may:

- Explain approved general definitions and source-provided categories.
- Summarize a currently approved notice with citation and timestamp.
- Suggest that travelers check official current information.

AI must not:

- Diagnose symptoms or assess an individual’s medical risk.
- Invent AQI, PM2.5, forecasts, source readings, or safe exposure time.
- Claim that an area or outdoor activity is safe.
- Recommend medication or protective equipment as individualized medical treatment.
- Transform a stale reading into current advice.
- override official notices or emergency instructions.

## Freshness and uncertainty

Every time-sensitive display must show source, observation/update time, geographic scope, metric, and freshness. When data is unavailable, conflicting, or stale, remove the current-value display and state that current conditions cannot be verified.

Proposed stale threshold must come from the selected provider’s update pattern and safety review; no universal threshold is set here.

## Provider outage

- Disable live readings and derived recommendations.
- Retain only reviewed seasonal information and approved external links.
- Do not substitute another unapproved provider or cached stale reading.
- Keep deterministic trip and emergency content available.
- Notify the founder when the outage affects an active service notice.

## Launch pause or limitation

The founder may limit pilot recruitment, pause marketing, suppress outdoor itinerary suggestions, or pause launch when an approved authority indicates severe conditions or when current conditions cannot be responsibly communicated.

No numeric pause threshold is approved. Before public launch, record:

- Authoritative trigger source
- Trigger category or measurement
- Who decides
- Affected features
- Communication text
- Reassessment cadence
- Resume criteria

Product operation may continue in a reduced mode if deterministic content remains safe and the limitation is clear.

## Founder escalation procedure

1. Confirm the source and timestamp independently.
2. Pause affected outdoor recommendations or current-value display.
3. Publish a short sourced notice with expiry.
4. Test English/Thai wording with the approved reviewer.
5. Monitor the approved authority at the chosen cadence.
6. Record decisions, changes, and user-impact evidence.
7. Resume only when the documented criteria pass.
8. Review any incorrect or stale communication as a safety incident.

## Founder decisions required

- Whether launch timing avoids the highest-risk seasonal period
- Whether reviewed seasonal content is included in the first slice
- Whether live air quality is deferred beyond pilot
- Approved authority/provider
- Pause, limitation, and resume triggers
- Qualified health/safety reviewer
- Responsibility and realistic monitoring hours

Until resolved, smoke-season readiness is **Evidence needed** and live air-quality functionality is not authorized.
