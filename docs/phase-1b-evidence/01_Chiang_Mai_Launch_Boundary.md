# Chiang Mai Launch Boundary

## Record status

- Status: **Proposed**
- Checked: 2026-07-29
- Reviewer: Codex, recommendation only
- Approval authority: Founder
- Evidence obtained: Phase 1 destination assessment
- Evidence needed: authoritative boundary data, field travel tests, emergency coverage mapping, and founder approval

## Boundary objective

Define a small, understandable central Chiang Mai service area that supports all MVP domains and can be verified by one founder. The data model remains nationwide; this boundary controls only operational publication and coverage claims.

## Proposed MVP area

The launch area should be one connected founder-approved polygon covering these proposed landmark zones:

- Chiang Mai Old City
- Night Bazaar and a limited riverside corridor
- Nimman and a limited Santitham corridor

These are descriptive proposals, not verified legal neighborhood or administrative boundaries. Inclusion must be decided through an approved map source and recorded polygon.

## Proposed travel limit

- Primary rule: records must fall inside the approved polygon.
- Proposed practical exception: a trip item may sit just outside the polygon only when it is reachable within approximately 20 minutes under ordinary conditions and has been individually approved.
- Proposed maximum radial guide: approximately 5 km from the future approved reference point.

Both time and distance are planning assumptions requiring field tests. Traffic varies, so neither is a travel guarantee. External navigation is handed to Google Maps or Apple Maps; the product does not calculate routes.

## Emergency-service radius

Emergency coverage may use a separate, larger service radius because the nearest appropriate hospital, fire station, police station, or assistance office may be outside the traveler-content polygon.

Before approval:

- Identify authoritative facilities serving the launch area.
- Verify phone, address, coordinates, service category, operating status, and jurisdiction.
- Test whether the boundary leaves meaningful gaps.
- Display clearly when a service is outside the content area.
- Do not imply that proximity guarantees suitability, capacity, response, or opening.

No emergency radius or facility is verified in this document.

## Explicit exclusions

- The full Mueang Chiang Mai district
- The full Chiang Mai municipality unless the approved polygon actually matches it
- Doi Suthep and other mountain destinations
- Mae Rim, Hang Dong, San Kamphaeng, and other surrounding districts
- Airport-only services unless individually approved
- Province-wide excursions
- Internal navigation, route timing, or transport guarantees
- Records without authorization, provenance, verification, and active publication state

## Verification implications

The founder must be able to:

- Visit or directly verify representative records in every included zone.
- Maintain ordinary volatile facts and stricter emergency cadences.
- Recheck recurring markets and near-term events.
- Review Thai/English names, addresses, and aliases.
- Process corrections, expiry, and takedown within the proposed capacity plan.

If the review queue exceeds the approved capacity, reduce the polygon or catalog before adding automation or staff.

## Smoke-season implications

Central Chiang Mai remains exposed to seasonal air-quality risk. Boundary approval does not establish safety. Launch timing and feature availability must follow `09_Smoke_Season_and_Service_Risk_Policy.md`. Live air-quality integration is outside the first thin slice unless separately approved.

## Boundary-change process

1. Submit a change proposal with purpose, source evidence, record counts, emergency impact, and weekly review cost.
2. Validate rights to any geographic data used.
3. Test travel expectations without promising route times.
4. Re-run destination activation standards for the added area.
5. Record founder approval and effective date.
6. Publish the coverage explanation before activating new records.
7. Preserve the previous boundary evidence for audit.
