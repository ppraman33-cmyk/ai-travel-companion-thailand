The documentation and evidence templates are complete, but Phase 1B requires real founder decisions, source permissions, emergency verification, provider reviews, asset rights, and capacity evidence. Templates are not evidence.

## 13. Blocking risks

- Rights cannot support storage, translation, or future commercial use.
- Emergency facts cannot be maintained at the proposed cadence.
- No fluent Thai safety reviewer or qualified legal reviewer is available.
- Exact boundary creates more content load than one founder can sustain.
- Provider terms or costs exceed accepted privacy/risk/budget.
- AI fails critical non-fabrication evaluation.
- Smoke-season operation lacks a defensible source and escalation rule.

- [ ] Founder approves a licensed exact boundary and service radius.
- [ ] Minimum catalog sources have concrete permission/license evidence.
- [ ] Rights reviewer approves every intended source class.
- [ ] Representative emergency records pass authoritative and secondary verification.
- [ ] Human emergency reviewer and cadence are approved.
- [ ] Initial real images and agreement have evidence and qualified review.
- [ ] Provider retention, regions, subprocessors, prices, restrictions, and exits are reviewed.
- [ ] English/Thai reviewer and evaluation set are available.
- [ ] Retention periods and user controls receive founder and qualified review.
- [ ] Token/load tests project below 70% of the applicable ceiling.
- [ ] Kill switches and deterministic fallback are specified for later testing.
- [ ] Four-week content review sample demonstrates sustainable capacity.
- [ ] Smoke-season source, triggers, messaging, and decision owner are approved.
- [ ] Every founder-register decision has a decision, date, conditions, and revisit date.
- [ ] No critical evidence record is merely a template or unsupported assumption.

No checklist item is complete solely because this document exists.

## 15. Next-phase recommendation

Remain in Phase 1B and perform founder-led evidence acquisition outside Codex. After every blocking checklist item has concrete evidence:

- **Proceed to logical database design** only if all critical rights, emergency, privacy, provider, budget, and capacity gates pass.
- **Proceed with conditions** only for noncritical residual items with named owners, deadlines, and no effect on architecture or safe publication.
- Otherwise **remain blocked** and reduce boundary, catalog, providers, or feature scope.

No application implementation should begin from the current state.
