# Chiang Mai Attractions Evidence Baseline — Research Closure

Status: `research_evidence_only`

Closure review: 2026-08-25

This report closes the district-by-district research pass. It does not claim
content completeness, redistribution clearance, current visitor operations or
publication readiness.

## Closure result

- Districts reviewed: 25/25
- Admitted evidence records: 23
- Accepted documented gaps: 2 (`5020` Wiang Haeng and `5021` Chai Prakan)
- Inherited records preserved by exact ID/name lock: 22/22
- Rights cleared: 0
- Media rights cleared: 0
- Publication eligible: 0

Mae Chaem closes with the Mae Hae Royal Project Development Centre. The direct
foundation page supplies identity, authority and the Mae Na Chon/Mae Chaem
address; the local authority lists it in its tourism section.

Chai Prakan remains a documented gap. The municipal tourism directory names
Ban Chiang Man Health Garden, while the maintenance evidence uses a different
Chaloem Phrakiat public-park identity. The reviewed official pages do not
establish that these names refer to the same attraction or jointly establish
current authority and exact parent for one stable identity. The Doi Wiang Pha
service unit and the wider multi-district park remain excluded.

Wiang Haeng remains a documented gap. Doi Dam is on the Wiang Haeng–Pai
boundary without a district-specific visitor entrance. The Black Gems route,
Naresuan route, broad historic areas and Paek Saem development station did not
jointly establish one current attraction identity, responsible authority and
exact visitor point suitable for this catalog.

## Revalidation triggers

This closure may be reopened only when at least one of these triggers exists:

- a new official source;
- new authority or ownership evidence;
- new exact district or visitor-point evidence; or
- an approved rights or freshness review.

It must not be reopened merely to force equal record counts.
