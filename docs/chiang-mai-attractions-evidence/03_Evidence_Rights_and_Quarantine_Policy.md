# Evidence, Rights, and Quarantine Policy

This baseline is an internal research aid, not an approved content catalog.

## Admission rule

A record requires a real named attraction, an official-source identity
assertion, a Chiang Mai district parent, and assertion-level source references.
Unknown English names, subdistricts, coordinates, opening hours, admission,
accessibility, ownership, and current operation remain explicitly pending.
Cross-district inference is prohibited.

## Rights rule

Facts are rewritten in project language. No source description, image, map,
document, or database extract is redistributed. Public accessibility is not a
license. All source/data rights remain pending, all media rights are unassessed,
and every record is publication-blocked.

## Quarantine rule

Files remain under `data/research`, `docs`, `scripts`, and `tests`. The registry
must not be imported by application, API, component, public-asset, sitemap,
manifest, service-worker, cache, database, seed, migration, RLS, RPC, or
generated-types code. The validator scans runtime paths and rejects leakage.

## Future exit gate

Publication requires a separate Founder decision after current official-source
verification, responsible-authority confirmation, rights review, freshness
review, field-level evidence completion, and media licensing. Merging this
research baseline would not grant production-import or redistribution approval.
