# Monetization and Cost Sustainability

## Product rule

Foreign tourists use AI Travel Companion Thailand for free. The current direction has no premium traveler tier and no unresolved free-versus-paid traveler decision.

## MVP priority

The MVP validates usefulness, trust, authorized data, emergency accuracy, and sustainable operating cost before introducing monetization. The traveler experience must not be designed around paid conversion.

## Cost sustainability without charging tourists

- Enforce per-user or per-device AI limits and global abuse controls.
- Bound input tokens, response size, itinerary size, latency, and concurrency.
- Set monthly budgets and warning thresholds for every metered provider.
- Prefer verified cached or deterministic content where license and freshness permit.
- Use the smallest capable model for bounded tasks.
- Limit the operational launch to a maintainable destination.
- Track cost per successful traveler task and per destination.
- Degrade gracefully while retaining verified records, saved content, emergency information, and map handoff.
- Add providers and infrastructure only when measured need justifies their fixed cost.

Temporary usage limits must not imply that the tourist can or must purchase access.

## Future monetization options

Monetization is post-MVP and requires separate approval. Possible models are limited to clearly disclosed:

- External affiliate referrals
- Business promotion
- Destination partnerships
- Aggregated business analytics
- Licensing approved companion capabilities

External accommodation or hotel affiliate links may be considered later. The product does not design or operate an internal hotel booking, inventory, checkout, payment, or reservation system in the MVP.

## Ranking integrity

Organic recommendations are produced from relevance, quality, traveler constraints, verification, and safety. Commercial payment must never silently change organic ranking. Any future promoted placement is:

- Clearly labeled at the point of decision
- Separable from organic recommendations
- Subject to normal safety, license, relevance, and quality gates
- Logged and measurable
- Prohibited from creating false scarcity or unsupported superiority

Emergency and tourist-assistance results cannot be sponsored or commercially reordered.

## Privacy and partner boundaries

Partners receive no privileged access to precise location, private itineraries, conversations, or personal profiles. Any future analytics uses appropriately aggregated or consented data and requires privacy, processor, retention, and legal review.

## Future approval requirements

Before any monetization experiment, approve:

- User benefit and disclosure standard
- Eligible and prohibited categories
- Organic-ranking separation
- Attribution, complaint, refund, and removal handling
- Partner verification and quality policy
- Privacy and data-sharing boundary
- Tax, consumer-protection, advertising, and licensing review
- Trust, safety, and cost guardrails

The project must not claim legal compliance until qualified review confirms applicable obligations.
