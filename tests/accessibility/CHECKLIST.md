# Accessibility foundation checklist

- Use semantic landmarks and heading order.
- Give interactive controls accessible names and visible focus states.
- Support keyboard-only operation without traps.
- Maintain WCAG AA color contrast for text and controls.
- Do not communicate status or meaning through color alone.
- Associate form labels, instructions, and errors with their controls.
- Announce asynchronous status changes appropriately.
- Provide text alternatives for meaningful images; mark decorative images as decorative.
- Respect zoom, text resizing, reduced motion, and responsive reflow.
- Test critical paths with automated checks and at least one screen reader.
- Verify language metadata and identify language changes in multilingual content.
- Keep emergency information readable, direct, and free of time-sensitive claims unless verified.
